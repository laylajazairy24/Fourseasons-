A year in Toronto

Four seasons, one city. Scroll down to see them.

Spring

Finally warm

Spring is finally here

The snow melts and everyone goes outside again. People go to High Park to see the cherry blossoms, even if it's still a bit cold.

Summer

Island season

Summer in the city

Summer is the best time in Toronto. People take the ferry to the Islands, go to the beach and sit on patios until late.

Fall

Back to school

The leaves change colour

School starts again and it gets colder. It's time for sweaters and a Tim Hortons coffee on the way to class.

Winter

Very cold

Winter is long

It snows a lot and it gets dark at 5pm. People go skating at Nathan Phillips Square and try to stay warm.

And then spring comes back.
